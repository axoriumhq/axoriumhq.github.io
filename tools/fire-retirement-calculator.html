What is FIRE?

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early. It is a movement dedicated to aggressive savings and smart investing so you can generate enough passive income to cover your living expenses forever, allowing you to quit your job decades before the traditional retirement age.

How the 4% Rule Works

The foundation of FIRE is the "4% Rule," which stems from the famous Trinity Study. It states that if you invest in a diversified portfolio, you can safely withdraw 4% of your total net worth every year without ever running out of money. Therefore, your "FIRE Target" is simply your annual expenses divided by 4% (or multiplied by 25).

Example Calculation

If you plan to spend $40,000 a year in retirement, you need a portfolio of $1,000,000 ($40,000 x 25). Once your investments reach $1 Million, the compound interest generated each year will theoretically pay for your $40,000 lifestyle indefinitely.
