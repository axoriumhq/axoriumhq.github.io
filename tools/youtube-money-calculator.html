How Do YouTubers Make Money?

The primary way creators make money on YouTube is through AdSense. When viewers watch ads before, during, or after a video, advertisers pay YouTube. YouTube then takes a 45% cut of that money and gives the creator the remaining 55%.

What is CPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) represents how much money advertisers are willing to pay for every 1,000 views on your video. Your CPM depends heavily on your channel's niche and audience demographics.

For example, a prank channel might have a very low CPM of $1.50 because advertisers don't want to spend much money to reach teenagers. However, a channel making videos about software, investing, or real estate could have a massive CPM of $15.00+ because their audience consists of adults with high purchasing power.

How Accurate is This Calculator?

This calculator provides a highly accurate estimate based on the raw metrics you input. However, it's important to remember that not every single view on a video gets an ad served to it (due to ad-blockers or skipped ads). Most creators use a conservative estimate to predict their actual take-home pay.
