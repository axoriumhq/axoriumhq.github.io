Why Crypto Calculations are Tricky

Unlike traditional stock brokers that now offer $0 commission trading, almost all cryptocurrency exchanges (like Binance, Coinbase, or Kraken) charge percentage-based Maker and Taker fees. This means you are taxed a percentage when you buy the coin, and taxed again when you sell it.

How Fees Eat Your Profits

If you invest $1,000 on an exchange with a 0.5% buy fee, you aren't actually buying $1,000 worth of crypto. The exchange takes $5 immediately, meaning only $995 is actually invested into the coin. When you sell, the fee is taken from your total gross value. This calculator runs the exact math to ensure you don't overestimate your take-home profit.

Understanding ROI

Return on Investment (ROI) is the universal metric for trading success. An ROI of 50% means you made half of your initial investment back in pure profit. By using this calculator to track your true ROI after fees, you can better manage your trading portfolio and set realistic take-profit targets.
