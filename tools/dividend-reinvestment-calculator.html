The Power of Reinvestment

Dividend reinvestment is often called the "Eighth Wonder of the World." Instead of taking your dividend checks as cash, you use them to buy more shares. Those shares, in turn, pay more dividends. Over 20 or 30 years, this creates an exponential growth curve that can turn a modest savings plan into a massive fortune.
