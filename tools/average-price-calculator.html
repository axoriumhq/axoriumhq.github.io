What is an Average Price Calculator?

An Average Price Calculator is a critical tool for stock market investors and cryptocurrency traders. It helps you determine your new break-even point when you buy more shares or coins of an asset you already own at a different price.

How "Averaging Down" Works

If you buy a stock at $100 and the price drops to $80, you are operating at a loss. If you buy more shares at the new, lower price of $80, your overall average cost per share drops below your original $100 entry. This strategy is known as "averaging down," and it allows you to become profitable faster when the stock eventually recovers.

The Formula

The math behind this calculator is a weighted average. It multiplies your existing quantity by your old price, adds it to your new quantity multiplied by the new price, and divides that total capital by your total number of shares.

Why Traders Need This Tool

Instead of guessing how much capital it will take to lower your entry price to a specific target, this tool instantly runs the exact numbers, allowing you to manage risk and deploy capital with precision.
