What is a Step-Up SIP?

A Step-Up Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows investors to increase their monthly investment amount by a fixed percentage every year. As your income grows, your investments should grow with it to beat inflation and achieve financial freedom faster.

Why is it Better Than a Standard SIP?

In a normal SIP, your investment amount remains flat for the entire duration. With a Step-Up SIP, even a conservative 10% annual increase in your investment can drastically multiply your final maturity value due to the power of compounding.

Example of the Step-Up Magic

If you invest 5,000 per month at a 12% return for 20 years, a standard SIP will yield around 50 Lakhs. However, if you apply a 10% annual Step-Up to that exact same SIP, your final value surges to nearly 1 Crore. Small, incremental increases make a massive difference over time.
